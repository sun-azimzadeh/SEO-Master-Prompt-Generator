# SEO Master Prompt Generator

An interactive SEO tool that asks you a series of questions and generates a complete SEO strategy along with optimized content.

## What it does

This tool collects your answers through a simple form and generates a full SEO package, including:

- Primary & secondary keywords
- Meta title
- Meta description
- Content outline
- Fully SEO-optimized article
- SEO strategy recommendations

## Features

- Simple form-based input
- Multi-language support (choose your preferred language)
- Generates complete SEO package in one go
- Creates ready-to-use meta tags
- Produces full optimized articles
- Provides strategic SEO recommendations
- Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI models

## How it works

1. Fill out the form
2. Select your desired language
3. Receive a complete SEO strategy and optimized article
